# Kristohanon SEO strategy

## Search-facing brand

**Kristohanon is a bilingual Catholic Scripture and prayer companion for
Filipinos.** It helps a person bring stress, grief, fear, family concerns, or a
desire to return to God into a private conversation grounded in reviewed Bible
passages, Catholic prayer, and practical next steps.

The brand must consistently sound gentle, trustworthy, Christ-centered, and
Filipino. It is a Scripture companion—not “AI Jesus,” clergy, therapy, or a
source of private revelation.

## Homepage search intent

Primary topic:

- Catholic Scripture and prayer in English and Cebuano

Supporting topics used naturally in headings, copy, links, and image text:

- Catholic prayer app for Filipinos
- Cebuano Bible verses
- English and Cebuano Catholic prayers
- Bible verses for anxiety, stress, grief, and hope
- how to pray the Rosary in English and Cebuano
- daily Bible verse and Catholic prayer
- Filipino Catholic Scripture companion

These phrases are editorial guidance, not a list to repeat mechanically. The
site does not use a `meta keywords` tag because major search engines do not use
it for ranking.

## Homepage on-page specification

- Title: `Catholic Scripture & Prayer in English and Cebuano | Kristohanon`
- H1: `Bring what you carry to Scripture and prayer.`
- Meta description: describes Catholic Bible verses, prayer, Rosary guidance,
  English/Cebuano language support, and the Filipino audience.
- One H1 only; major page chapters use H2 and card subsections use H3.
- Canonical URL comes from `APP_URL` so a future domain migration can be made
  without editing page templates.
- WebSite and WebApplication JSON-LD identify the product, publisher, language,
  free access, official logo, and contact email.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata use the homepage devotional image.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` expose public routes while excluding private
  application and storage paths.

## Content priorities

Future search growth should come from useful, original pages rather than
keyword repetition. Strong candidates are English/Cebuano prayer guides,
Rosary mystery explainers, and pastoral Scripture collections for specific
needs. Every page should answer one clear question, cite its sources, use a
unique title and description, and link naturally to the relevant prayer or
Scripture experience.
